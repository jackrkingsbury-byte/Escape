Chess apps on your phone

A small ♞ button floats over every app. Open your chess app, tap it, and the best move pops up right there. Android asks once to allow “Display over other apps” and to share your screen.

Android — a floating Scan button over any app
  1. Download the Chess Lens app (APK).
  2. Open the download. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps.
  3. In the app tap Start the floating Scan button, allow Display over other apps, then Start now to share the screen.
  4. Open your chess app and tap the ♞ button. The best move pops up on top.

Without installing: take a screenshot, then use Open image here.

iPhone — double-tap the back of your phone

Apple doesn't let any app read another app's screen, so Chess Lens uses a shortcut: a double-tap screenshots your chess app and opens Chess Lens.

  1. Open the Shortcuts app, tap + and add 3 actions: Take Screenshot, Copy to Clipboard, Open URLs with . Name it “Chess Lens”.
  2. Settings → Accessibility → Touch → Back Tap → Double Tap → pick “Chess Lens”.
  3. In your chess app, double-tap the back of the phone. In Chess Lens tap Read screenshot, then Paste.
