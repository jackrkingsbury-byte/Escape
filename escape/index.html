    What this is. A pattern scanner, not a lawyer. It finds clause types that commonly cost people money and shows you the sentence each one came from, so you can check it yourself against your document.
    
  
    What it is not. It is not legal advice, and it does not know your circumstances or your country's consumer law. It can miss things — especially in a document you have pasted only part of. Every finding quotes your own text, so verify each one before you rely on it. For anything large, expensive or already in dispute, get a professional to read it.
    
  
    Privacy. There is no server. The contract never leaves this page — the scan, the letter and the card are all built in your browser. The share link contains only the score and the list of clause types found.
