How the maths works (and where to double-check it)

Squad rating. Sum the 11 ratings, take the average, add up how far each player sits above that average, add that excess to the sum, divide by 11, round down. Autopilot shows the exact decimal so you can see how tight a squad is — anything landing on .0x is worth double-checking in game, or tick the safety margin option.

Chemistry. Modern rules: a player earns points only when played in a position he's listed for, and points come from how many squadmates share his club, league and nation — capped at 3 per player, 33 for the squad.

  • Club: 2 players → 1, 4 → 2, 7 → 3
  • League: 3 players → 1, 5 → 2, 8 → 3
  • Nation: 2 players → 1, 5 → 2, 8 → 3
  • Icons: 3 chemistry in position, and count double toward their nation.
  • Heroes: 3 chemistry in position, and count double toward their league.

Out-of-position players still count toward everyone else's club, league and nation tallies — they just earn nothing themselves.

Card quality is read from rating: bronze under 65, silver 65–74, gold 75+.

The search is a randomised swap search with restarts, not an exhaustive proof. It finds cheap passing squads fast, but a cheaper one may exist — raise the effort setting and run it again if the price looks high.
