# Season 2 production line

Posting: every finished episode goes straight to TikTok (@alexlivealert) through Buffer with `mode: shareNow`,
`schedulingType: automatic`, `isAiGenerated: false`, caption = "Episode N 🔥 <story hook>" + exactly 5 hashtags:
`#animation #robloxanimation #storytime #huss #funny`. Videos are copied to `videos/` (public raw links).

Rules from the creator:
- The narrator is always the creator's avatar: cast `me` = `MAIN.me` (black hoodie, knight emblem, headphones).
- Original stories only (borrow the shape of the reference videos, never their plots).
- Background music by vibe: funny / meme / homey / trend = the bouncy reference loop (`party`);
  mysterious / mystical = quiet (`night`, `lofi`, silence at reveals); spooky = `spooky` drone, silence before scares.
- Meme episodes: series shout-outs as quick beats with a boom/scratch + stamped word ("Ah, almost... almost!",
  "Goon Squad", "while they're out partying, by the way", "Top ten respect", new nonsense chants like
  "Saibao! Ela des! Ela sobi! Nubani!"). Never the word "gooning".
- Quality: rich sets, extras, mood lighting, fog/particles, smooth camera moves, screen shake on hits.
